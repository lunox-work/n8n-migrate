# Bench corpus: exclusion rules

Fixed on 2026-11-14, before the corpus was fetched or any template was labelled. `evals/bench/fetch.mjs` applies them in this order (`excludeReason()`); the first rule that fires is written to `corpus.json` as `excluded`. An excluded template counts toward nothing: neither the 100 eligible, nor the labelled pool. It still counts in "coverage of the top 100" when that is reported raw.

| Rule          | Excluded when                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Why                                   |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| `paid`        | the listing entry has `price > 0` or a `purchaseUrl`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | the workflow is not freely available  |
| `unavailable` | the template download fails after 3 tries, or `.workflow.workflow` has no `nodes` array or no `connections` object                                                                                                                                                                                    | nothing to run                        |
| `notes-only`  | no node that is enabled and not a sticky note                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | only sticky notes                     |
| `no-edges`    | at least 2 enabled non-sticky nodes and no `main` connection between any two of them                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | a node gallery, not a workflow        |
| `duplicate`   | its sha256 (`canonical()` of `.workflow.workflow`) equals that of a template earlier in the listing                                                                                                                                                                                                   | counted once                          |
| `tutorial`    | the analyzer (default answers) gives a `not-an-automation` blocker, or an enabled node is an n8n training node (`n8nTrainingCustomerDatastore`, `n8nTrainingCustomerMessenger`), or the name matches `TUTORIAL_NAME` (below)                                                                          | a tutorial or demo about n8n itself   |
| `hand`        | excluded by reading the template, for one of the reasons above that the mechanical rules missed. Only allowed with a one-line reason in `corpus.json`, decided from the template alone (never from the analyzer's outcome), and only for templates that are tutorials, demos of n8n, or not workflows | the regexes cannot see every tutorial |

`TUTORIAL_NAME` (case-insensitive), applied to the template name:

- any of the words `tutorial`, `course`, `lesson`, `beginner`, `beginners`, `getting started`, `101`, `walkthrough`, `cheat sheet`, `cheatsheet`, `playground`; or
- the word `n8n` together with any of `learn`, `learning`, `guide`, `demo`, `example`, `examples`, `basics`, `intro`, `introduction`, `explained`, `features`, `how to`.

Names that contain `demo` or `example` without `n8n` are not excluded, only flagged `flag: "name-demo"` in `corpus.json`, so a reader can apply `hand` with a reason.

Not excluded on purpose, because they are real refusals the bench should measure: community or unmapped nodes, the n8n API node, error workflows, synchronous webhooks, public chat pages, disabled triggers, and templates whose only trigger is manual.

## Held-out

`splits.json` holds `dev` (60) and `held-out` (40). A session that changes the skill, the converter or the runtime must never read the current round's held-out templates, their fixtures or their results; they run only at release points (PLAN Phase 6). Held-out templates already scored at a release point join dev in the next round (`rounds`, rolling held-out) and may be read from then on; round 2's held-out was held-out 21–40 (release point 2). From round 3 every seeded template is dev; round 3's held-out is the 20 fresh templates of `splits.json` `heldOutFresh` (above), and the same rule covers them, their fixtures, their results, their hand labels (`labels-held3.json`, `LABELS-HELD3.md`) and `RELEASE-*.md`. Round 4's held-out (`splits.json` `heldOutFresh4`, "Round 4 held-out" below) falls under the same rule from the moment it is drawn, while round 3 is still current. That covers its templates, fixtures, results and hand labels (`labels-held4.json`, `LABELS-HELD4.md`). Round 5 (after release point 4) promotes them to dev, so from then on they and RELEASE-4.md's aggregates may be read. No corpus-wide folder or report a fixing session can read holds a current held-out template (PROPOSALS P51): the survey corpus `evals/survey/data/wf` never does (`tests/bench-fixtures.test.mjs` checks it where the folder exists; the top-up skips survey templates), and `convert-coverage.mjs` without `--split` or `--ids` skips the current round's held-out ids and reports only their count.

## Round 4 eligibility

Fixed on 2026-11-15, before it was applied to any template. The corpus exists so the loop covers as much conversion as possible, so every template in it must be workable end to end: it runs on Claude Code surfaces, n8n gives it a baseline on the bench, and there is something to compare. A template is eligible for round 4 (dev and held-out alike) when all of the clauses below hold. Each is decided from the template file (`evals/bench/data/wf`), the analyzer with default answers (the checkout's analyzer, the fetch's `--now`) and the committed lists in `evals/bench/eligibility.json`, never from a bench result, a fixture or a pass/fail. `evals/bench/eligible.mjs` applies them (`checkEligibility()`, unit-tested in `tests/bench-eligible.test.mjs`); a template fails at every clause it breaks, and all are reported. Steps checked are the analyzer's steps on branch `main` (what a run from the analyzer's wake-up reaches, sub-nodes and self-called sub-workflows included; disabled nodes left out).

1. **Exclusions and outcome.** It passes the rules above (no `excluded` in `corpus.json`); the analyzer says `subscription`; every wake-up in `wakeup.all` is `manual`, `schedule`, `poll`, `chat`, `form` or `subworkflow` (no relay, endpoint, MCP server or error trigger); no other trigger's target is `hosted` or `keep-in-n8n`; and no requirement is a relay.
2. **In scope for v1.** No step on branch `main` or `error` matches `outOfScope`, PLAN "Out of scope for v1" pinned to node types and parameters: a Respond to Webhook node or a Webhook with `responseMode` `lastNode`/`responseNode`/`streaming` (synchronous replies); a Chat Trigger with `public: true` (a public chat page, whatever the default answer to who uses it); a Wait with `resume` `webhook` or `form`, or a Form node's `page` operation (waits on a webhook); any `sendAndWait` operation; an Error Trigger; a persistent-connection trigger (IMAP, MQTT, Kafka, AMQP, RabbitMQ, Redis, Postgres, local file, SSE); an Execute Workflow or Workflow tool whose target is not the same workflow (`={{ $workflow.id }}`) or inline (sub-workflow missing from the export); a community node with no entry in `features`; a shortest schedule or poll interval under 60 s.
3. **n8n gives a baseline.** (a) n8n does not fail on the template itself: no main-branch node type missing from `references/nodes.json` (the n8n node packages) unless a bench fix installs it, and no main-branch node matching `n8nSelfFail` (required parameters left empty that n8n 2.47.7 refuses, a chat model in the completion class), each rule naming the refusal it comes from. (b) Every main-branch step's outside service is stood in for today (`bench`: runs inside n8n, HTTP through the mock, a model the shim answers, a node pin) or by a **bounded** bench fix in `bench.fixes` (a sidecar, an image flag, a credential dummy); the template is then eligible and reports the fix as pending. A step that needs a large unbuilt bench capability (`none`, or unlisted) makes it ineligible. `expect.n8nFails` and `expect.n8nCannotRun` marks are a consistency check of this clause, not its source.
4. **Planned features.** Every main-branch step's node type (and operation where listed) maps in `features` to a feature the plan intends to build, built or not (database steps through stored statements, vector stores, media, files, app REST request templates, approved substitutions, state); unlisted or `null` makes it ineligible.
5. **Something to compare.** The main branch reaches a write, send or media step to an outside service (analyzer `effect: write` or kind `media`, on the kinds in `comparable.effectKinds`), or the wake-up is `chat` (the reply), or a terminal step whose output items P70 compares (role `step`, no main connection to another main-branch step, type not in `comparable.nonComparedTerminals`).
6. **Hand.** A template whose own configuration makes n8n fail in a way the lists cannot see (wiring, an expression over a step's input) is ineligible only with a one-line reason in its `corpus.json` entry (`r4Hand`), decided from the template alone, never from a bench result.

Changing a list is a commit made before it is applied, like this section. Strata are the fetch's (`chat`, `manual`, `schedule`, `form`, `poll`, `other`).

**Empty fields (clause 3a; PROPOSALS P91, written 2026-11-16 for later rounds; round 4's audit and corpus stay as applied).** An empty required field that names the person's own account resource (a mail sender or recipient, a label, a spreadsheet, document or folder id, a chat or channel id, a Graph object id) is a placeholder: the overlay fills it on both sides and the template keeps its baseline. An empty field that holds content or configuration (the text to post, the columns to match on, an API version, a tool's language) is self-failing configuration. Checked against `n8nSelfFail`: the post text, matching columns and Graph API version rules are content or configuration and stand; the Facebook Graph API `node` rule names the person's Graph object, so it moves to placeholder in the next round's lists (4352 stays out in round 4 on its empty API version alone).

**Pending list changes for the next round** (found by R4e's canned checks; each becomes a list commit before the next round's eligibility is applied, never a bench-result exclusion of a round-4 template): a main-branch Python Code node written for the Pyodide runtime n8n 2.0 removed (`_input`, `micropip`, top-level `await`, JsProxy `.to_py()`) is self-failing (PROPOSALS P95; 1992, 2374); a main-branch JavaScript Code node that `require()`s a module is self-failing on the default image (P87); uProc's `language` is required for its text, audio and country tools in n8n 2.47.7 (1073); the Facebook Graph API `node` rule moves to placeholder (above); `bench.loopbackHosts` keeps only `localhost` as P52 (built), since `127.0.0.1` and `::1` still never reach the mock. From R4j (P104): a main-branch HTTP Request whose JSON body splices an expression raw into a JSON string literal while that value always holds a line break or a quote by the template's own construction (4846: Set data writes `\n\n` into `prompt`) is self-failing.

## Round 4 eligibility, amendment 1

Fixed on 2026-11-16 and committed before it was applied (the same commit holds the lists, the node-description extract, the check and its tests). Why: bench work after the round-4 audit found templates that fail in n8n 2.47.7 on their own configuration, which the first `n8nSelfFail` clauses missed (PROPOSALS P91, P92, P93, P95, and canned checks of round-4 templates). The maintainer's direction stands: every round-4 corpus template must be workable end to end. The new clauses belong to clause 3a. Like the others they are decided from the template file alone, here with n8n's own node descriptions, never from a bench result, a fixture or a pass/fail. A node with pinned data is not checked, since n8n checks none. Each reason carries `amendment: 1` (`eligibility.json` `n8nSelfFail.amendment1`; `eligible.mjs`; `tests/bench-eligible.test.mjs`).

- **(a) A required parameter left empty.** A main-branch node's parameter fails when n8n's node description marks it required (the version group of the node's `typeVersion`), n8n shows it for the node's parameters (displayOptions, with defaults filled in), and its value is empty the way n8n counts it (`addToIssuesIfMissing`: string, options or date '' or unset, multi-options `[]`, a resource locator without a value). n8n then refuses the run with `Parameter "…" is required`. The descriptions are in `evals/bench/eligibility-params.json`, extracted by `eligible.mjs --extract` from `n8n-nodes-base` and `@n8n/n8n-nodes-langchain` 2.47.5 (the versions n8n 2.47.7 ships). The extract reads only the packages' `dist/types/nodes.json` and covers the node types in the cached corpus and gallery templates. P91's placeholders do not count: a resource locator, a workflow selector, a value n8n loads from the person's account, and the string fields `requiredEmpty.placeholders` lists (a sender or recipient, a chat, a spreadsheet, document or list id, the person's own webhook). Some content fields are refused by the node's code although the description does not mark them required. Each of those is a list rule with `amendment: 1` naming the refusal (LinkedIn's post text: `The Text field is empty`).
- **(b) Python the native runner refuses.** A main-branch Code node (or Code tool) in Python runs on n8n 2.x's native runner (the runners image, standard library only), because n8n 2.0 removed Pyodide. It fails when its code, without comments and string literals, uses `micropip`, `pyodide`, the `js` bridge, `.to_py()` or `_input`, has an `await` outside an `async def`, or imports a module outside Python's standard library (`stdlib`) other than inside a `try:` block (P95).
- **(c) A prompt taken from a chat input nothing supplies.** A main-branch model, chain, assistant or agent node can take its prompt from its input's `chatInput`: a prompt type `auto`, a prompt left at its `={{ $json.chatInput }}` default, or a prompt expression naming `$json.chatInput`. It fails (`No prompt specified`) when no main-path ancestor can supply that field. A chat trigger can, and so can a sub-workflow trigger (its caller's fields are not in the template) and an ancestor whose parameters or pinned data name `chatInput`.

It takes two items off the pending list above: Pyodide-era Python (P95) and uProc's empty language (through (a)). The others stay pending. The Facebook Graph API `node` field is listed as a placeholder for (a), but its round-4 list rule stands. A wiring failure the lists cannot see stays a hand call (clause 6, `r4Hand`), decided from the template alone.

**Applying it.** The amended rule is applied with `eligible.mjs` to round 4's dev and held-out, and with `eligible.mjs --reapply-walk 4` to the round-4 walk's candidates. A walk entry that only the amendment makes ineligible keeps pool `fresh` and its place in the seeded shuffle. It gets `r4: "ineligible"`, `r4Reasons` and `r4Amended: 1`, and the draw skips it like a template excluded after the draw (`fetch.mjs` `isCandidate`). Counts stay as in "Round 4 held-out", dev 100 and held-out 20:

- **Held-out.** A held-out template that now fails is redrawn by the committed procedure: `split.mjs --fresh --round 4 --redraw "<why>"`, the same seed, and the next candidate in its stratum's shuffled order, recorded in `heldOutFresh4.redraws`. A candidate named in a file fixing sessions read (`levers.json`, `LEVERS.md`) is no longer fresh ("Later rounds' held-out" below) and is passed over. If a stratum runs out, the walk continues under "Round 4 held-out": pages 5–6 first, then past the ranked gallery pages with the same rules, at least 1 s apart, cached.
- **Dev.** Each dev template that now fails leaves (round 4 `dropped`). Its place goes to the walk's next candidate in the same stratum, in the same shuffled order continued past the held-out's draws (`devAdded`). If the stratum has none left, it goes to the next eligible entry of the views walk after page 6, under the same rules, skips and analyzer, finishing the listing page (`split.mjs --amend 1 --round 4`, added when it was applied). New dev and redrawn held-out templates have no fixtures until the blind session writes them.

## Round 4 held-out

Fixed on 2026-11-15, before any round-4 template was fetched or opened. Seed **20261116**. Round 4's held-out is 20 templates nobody has read: none from the Phase 5 overflow or the round-3 candidates left undrawn, whose verdicts are in `corpus.json`.

1. **Walk.** `node evals/bench/fetch.mjs --top-up --round 4` reads the same listing (views, descending, 50 rows a page). It starts at the page holding the highest rank an earlier walk read. It applies the rules above, unchanged and in the same order, with the same analyzer settings (default answers, `--now 2026-11-16T00:00:00Z`, the checkout's analyzer). It keeps the same two skips: ids already in `corpus.json`, and templates in `evals/survey/data/wf` (P51). Requests are at least 1 s apart, with 3 tries and backoff. Template JSON stays git-ignored. Every new entry that no rule excludes also gets the round-4 eligibility verdict ("Round 4 eligibility" above, `eligible.mjs` `checkEligibility()`, `eligibility.json` as committed). It is written to `corpus.json` as `r4` (`eligible`/`ineligible`) with `r4Reasons`. New entries carry `topUp: 4`, and their pool is `fresh` only when eligible.
2. **Stop.** The walk stops once every wake-up stratum has its quota of eligible round-4 candidates (chat 6, manual 5, schedule 4, poll 2, form 2, other 1), but first finishes the listing page it is on. The common strata therefore have more candidates than slots, and the draw is a real draw.
3. **Draw.** `node evals/bench/split.mjs --fresh --round 4` uses round 3's method with this seed. It is a mulberry32 Fisher-Yates shuffle over the eligible round-4 entries sorted by id ascending, and each stratum takes its first quota in shuffled order. A template excluded after the draw (rule `hand`, or `r4Hand`) stays in the shuffle, and only its stratum moves to the next candidate (`--redraw`, recorded). The result goes to `splits.json` `heldOutFresh4`. Round 4 in `rounds`:

   - **promoted:** round 3's 20 held-out (scored at release point 3).
   - **dropped:** the 20 of those 120 that fail round-4 eligibility (AUDIT-R4.md), so dev is the 100 eligible.
   - **held-out:** the 20 drawn.

   `currentRound` becomes 4 only after release point 3 is committed. Until then, the P51 guards also skip every later round's held-out.

   The walk (2026-11-15) read listing pages 5 and 6 (100 entries; 2 listing and 72 template requests). It skipped 24 entries already in `corpus.json` and none from the survey folder. Of the 76 new entries, 4 were excluded `paid`, with no `tutorial`, `hand` or `duplicate`. 36 failed round-4 eligibility; counting each clause they break, these are outcome 26, relay or hosted 21, feature 30, synchronous webhook reply 9, sub-workflow missing 4, send-and-wait 3, public chat page 3, nothing to compare 3, community node 2, persistent connection 2, bench 2, wait on webhook 1 and required parameter empty 1. That left 36 candidates: chat 7, manual 14, schedule 9, poll 3, form 2, other 1. `split.mjs --fresh --round 4` drew 20 of them before any template was opened.

4. **Labels.** Hand labels go in `labels-held4.json` and `LABELS-HELD4.md`. They are written from the template JSON alone, before anyone looks at the analyzer's output for these templates or reads their `corpus.json` entries. Then `compare-labels.mjs --labels evals/bench/labels-held4.json` runs. The labels are held-out material, like `labels-held3.json`. Result (labels committed in c25dfb8 before the analyzer ran on these templates; `LABELS-HELD4.md`): outcome 20 of 20, outcome and target 19, wake-up 20, blockers 20, questions 20, everything 19. In the one disagreement the analyzer is right under `capabilities.json`: a model step needs `sample` on the artifact surface, so a model-only manual run is a skill. No template was excluded or redrawn while labelling.

## Later rounds' held-out: freshness after the gallery ranking

Fixed on 2026-11-16. `evals/bench/levers.mjs` (LEVERS.md) has run the analyzer and the converter coverage check over the listing's first 20 pages (top ~1,000 by views) and names their in-scope ids in files fixing sessions read. Those templates are no longer fresh. A later round's held-out walk (`fetch.mjs --top-up --round N`, N ≥ 5) therefore skips every id on the listing pages `levers.json` `listingPages` records, in addition to the skips above, and starts after them. The ranking itself is only re-run `--offline` from its cache, never on new listing pages, so it never reads a future held-out candidate; extending it to more pages first moves the held-out walk's start past them.
